                Two Ways Your AI Uses Files
                
                      
                
                        When you upload documents, they appear in the Files section at the top.
                        From there you decide how the AI should use them — keep them as Direct File Access, move them to Vector Search, or both.
                      
                

                      
                      
                
                        
                 Direct File Access — Read & Edit
                
                        
                
                          Files in the top section are stored as-is. The AI can open, read, edit, and quote them directly — like
                          handing someone a physical document. This is ideal when you need the AI to work with the exact content of a file:
                          tables, formatting, specific pages, or full text.
                        
                
                        
                  
                          
                • Best for: spreadsheets, structured data (CSV/Excel), small documents you want read in full, files you want the AI to edit or transform.
                  • 
                          
                • How it works: the AI opens the file, extracts the content, and reads it into its context — similar to you opening a file on your computer.
                  • 
                          
                • Limitation: the AI reads one file at a time, and very large files may be truncated. It can't search across multiple files at once.
                  • 
                        
                
                      
                

                      
                      
                
                        
                 Vector Search — Semantic Search (Vector)
                
                        
                
                          Files moved to the bottom section get everything from Direct File Access (read & edit) plus
                          semantic search. Their content is split into chunks, converted into vector embeddings, and stored in a search database.
                          When you ask a question, the AI can search across all indexed files at once and retrieve the most relevant passages —
                          while still being able to open and edit the full files.
                        
                
                        
                  
                          
                • Best for: large documents, multiple documents on the same topic, Q&A over a knowledge base, finding specific information buried in long texts.
                  • 
                          
                • How it works: your question is converted into the same vector format, then matched against all stored chunks by meaning (not just keywords). The closest matches are pulled into the AI's context.
                  • 
                          
                • Limitation: only the matching chunks are retrieved — not the entire document. Broad questions like "summarize everything" won't work well because there's no single chunk that matches that intent.
                  • 
                        
                
                        
                
                          External sources ( URLs,
                           JIRA,
                           GitHub) are automatically placed in Vector Search because their content is fetched remotely and only meaningful once indexed.
                        
                
                        
                
                          
                          
                How Vector Search works under the hood (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
                
                        
                
                      
                

                      
                      
                
                        
                When to Use Which?
                
                        
                
                          
                            
                
                              
                               Direct File Access
                               Vector Search
                            
                
                                          
                          
                            
                
                              Read full document
                              ✅ Yes
                              ❌ Only matching chunks
                            
                
                            
                
                              Search across many files
                              ❌ One at a time
                              ✅ All at once
                            
                
                            
                
                              Tables & structured data
                              ✅ Preserves structure
                              ⚠️ Structure may be lost in chunks
                            
                
                            
                
                              Find a specific fact
                              ⚠️ Must know which file
                              ✅ Finds it automatically
                            
                
                            
                
                              Large / many documents
                              ⚠️ Slow, may truncate
                              ✅ Scales well
                            
                
                                          
                        
                
                        
                
                          Tip: You can place a file in both sections. Keep it in Direct File Access for full-document access,
                          and also move it to Vector Search so its content is searchable. The AI will pick the best approach automatically.
                        
                
                      
                

                      
                      
                
                        
                Moving Files Between Sections
                
                        
                Use the toolbar between the two sections:
                
                        
                  
                          
                •  Down arrow — Enable Vector Search for selected files (Direct Access → Vector Search). The files are processed and become searchable.
                  • 
                          
                •  Up arrow — Use Direct Access only (Vector Search → Direct Access). Removes them from the search database; the original file stays.
                  • 
                          
                •  Refresh — Re-index all Vector Search files. Use this after changing chunk settings or if content was updated at the source.
                  •

                 Vector Search Settings
                
                        
                These only affect Vector Search. Click the settings icon to configure:
                
                        
                  
                          
                • 
                            Chunk Size — How documents are split before indexing.
                            
                    
                              
                  • Smaller chunks (200–500 tokens): better for precise factual retrieval.
                    • 
                              
                  • Larger chunks (1 000+ tokens): better for preserving context in complex documents.
                    • 
                            
                  
                          
                  • 
                          
                • 
                            Chunk Count — How many chunks are retrieved per question.
                            
                    
                              
                  • More chunks: broader coverage, but may include less relevant passages.
                    • 
                              
                  • Fewer chunks: sharper focus, but might miss relevant details.
                    • 
                            
                  
                          
                  • 
                        
                
                        
                After changing settings, press  to re-index so changes take effect.
                
                      
                

                      
                      
                
                        
                Best Practices for Vector Search
                
                        
                
                          Vector Search finds passages by meaning, not keywords. It works best with focused questions that match the kind of language in your documents.
                        
                
                        
                  
                          
                • Ask specific questions — "What was the Q3 revenue?" beats "Tell me about the financials."
                  • 
                          
                • Break complex queries into smaller ones — search twice rather than asking one vague question.
                  • 
                          
                • Use the same terminology your documents use — technical terms, project names, etc.
                  • 
                        
                

                        
                
                          
                Instead of: "Tell me everything about the project"
                
                          
                Try: "What are the main objectives of Project Alpha?" → then "What risks were identified for Project Alpha?"

                 About Tools & MCP
                
              
              
                
                
                 What are Tools?
                
                
                Tools allow your AI assistant to perform specific actions for you. Think of them as special abilities that let your assistant:
                
                
                  
                  
                • Search the web for information
                  • 
                  
                • Create and modify files
                  • 
                  
                • Connect to your enterprise applications
                  • 
                  
                • Generate images or analyze data
                  • 
                
                
                
                When you enable a tool, you're giving your assistant permission to use that capability during your conversation.
                
              
                
              
              
                
                
                 What is MCP?
                
                
                MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a way to extend what your AI assistant can do by connecting to external services.
                
                
                In simple terms:
                
                
                  
                  
                • MCP servers are like specialized "skill packs" for your AI
                  • 
                  
                • They let your assistant access additional capabilities not built into the base system
                  • 
                  
                • Examples include connecting to your Google Calendar, accessing specific databases, or interacting with custom applications
                  • 
                
                
                
                By adding MCP servers, you're essentially giving your AI assistant new ways to help you with specific tasks.

            What are AI Models?
          
          
                Understanding AI Models
                
          
                AI models are large language models (LLMs) trained on vast amounts of text data. Each model has different capabilities, strengths, and limitations.
                

          
                What knowledge do models have?
                
          
                Models have general knowledge from their training data up to their cutoff date. They don't have:
                
          
                  
            
                • Real-time information beyond their training cutoff
                  • 
            
                • Access to private company data unless specifically provided
                  • 
            
                • Ability to browse the internet on their own
                  • 
          
                

          
                How to choose the right model?
                
          
                Consider these factors when selecting a model:
                
          
                  
            
                • Context window: How much text the model can process at once
                  • 
            
                • Specialties: Some models excel at coding, reasoning, or creative tasks
                  • 
            
                • Response quality: More advanced models generally provide more accurate and nuanced responses
                  •
